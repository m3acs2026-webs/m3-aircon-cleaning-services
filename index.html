Good to know

Questions, answered.

How often should I have my aircon cleaned?

For most homes, every 3–6 months is a good starting point. High-use rooms, pets, and dusty areas may benefit from more frequent cleaning.

How long does a cleaning take?

A standard split-type unit typically takes 30–60 minutes, depending on its condition and access.

Do you provide a workmanship warranty?

Yes. Every service includes a written workmanship warranty. We confirm the coverage in your quote.
